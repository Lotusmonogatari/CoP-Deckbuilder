## 1. Decisions recorded (Cameron, 2026-11-17)

| # | Decision |
|---|---|
| D1 | The game has three UI styles. **Living Office** is the default and is free. **Broadcast** and **Woodblock** are unlocked by purchase. The player can switch between any styles they own at any time. |
| D2 | **Broadcast keeps today's gold lines**: the thin gold border on the top and bottom of the support bar (`SlantBar`), the gold threshold mark, and the gold top and bottom lines of the cue band (`CueBanner`). |
| D3 | **Every style keeps the existing suit card fronts and backs exactly.** That means `CardView.gd`, `CardBackView.gd`, `data/art.json` "card_frames" and the card art, unchanged. A style may change only what sits *around* a card: the hand's arrangement, the zoom's backdrop and buttons. |
| D4 | The Office's dues display becomes **End of Week Payments**: party dues and staff wages in one total, with its parts shown. See the companion spec. |

## 2. What a style is

A style is the **look and arrangement** of the screens. It never changes rules, numbers, what the player is told, or wording. Same game, same information; a different room around it.

| Changes with the style | Never changes with the style |
|---|---|
| Colours, fonts, panel shapes, motifs (gold lines, seals, wood) | Card fronts and backs (D3) |
| How menus are presented (buttons, room objects, plaques) | Every number, rule and Text row |
| How the hand is arranged (overlap, fan) | What Information Standards require on each screen |
| How the support count is drawn (slanted bar, framed bar, seats) | Intent, energy, guard and gaffes shown before you act |
| The card zoom's backdrop and buttons | Drag up 220 px to play; tap to zoom |
| The levels list's presentation (rows, prints, folders) | The reason a level can't be started |

## 3. How it fits the existing systems

Use the **cosmetic package** system (`scripts/rules/CosmeticPieces.gd`, `data/cosmetic_packages.json`, the Appearance panel). It already handles buying something once and equipping one per slot.

- **New slot:** `ui_style` in `CosmeticPieces.SLOTS`. A package that carries a value in a new **`UI Style`** column on the Cosmetic Packages tab provides that slot. Values: `broadcast`, `woodblock`. *(This is a new column, so ask Cameron first.)*
- **Default:** no `ui_style` equipped means Living Office. Living Office is not a package, so it can't be un-owned.
- **New packages:** two rows, Broadcast and Woodblock, priced by Cameron (open question Q1).
- **Where to buy and switch:**
  - **Buy** in the Marketplace, on a new **Office styles** tab (mockup `C_Styles`). It lists the same packages and uses the same `CosmeticPieces.buy_refusal()` and costs.
  - **Switch** from that tab, and in Administration › Appearance and Music, which already equips per slot.
- **State:** `GameState.active_cosmetics["ui_style"]` is saved with the run, as the other slots are. Ask Cameron whether the style should instead follow the device, like the text size in `user://settings.json` (Q2).
- **Tokens:** a new workbook tab, **UI Styles**, with one row per style and token (colour, font role, size, radius), exported to `data/ui_styles.json`. Starting values are in `ui_styles_tokens_2026-11-17.json`. *(This is a new tab, so ask Cameron first.)*
- **Themes:** `tools/build_theme.gd` builds one theme per style from the tokens: `theme/cop_theme_living.tres`, `_broadcast.tres`, `_woodblock.tres`. Today's `cop_theme.tres` becomes the Broadcast base, since Broadcast is the closest to today's look.
- **Presentation:**
  - One static helper, `UiStyle.current()`, returns the active style id.
  - Presenters that differ by style ask it and build their variant. They are `OfficeScreen`'s menu, `SupportBar`, `CueBanner`, `HandPresenter`, the zoom chrome in `BattleScreen` and the levels list.
  - Rules stay in `scripts/rules/`. Nothing in `scripts/rules/` may read the style.
- **Switching mid-run:** it takes effect on the next screen change. A battle in progress keeps its style until it ends.

## 5. Screens in this pass

**Seats (Living Office).** Twenty seats in two rows (12 outer, 8 inner), filled left to right: player, then undecided, then opponent. Each seat stands for 5 people, rounded to the nearest seat, with the exact numbers printed underneath ("You 30 · Undecided 25 · Ayala 45"). The threshold sits in the centre ("80 to advance"). The seat count is a Balance row, so it can be tuned.

**Hand (all styles).** Real `CardView` fronts at today's card size, overlapped so every card in hand is visible for hand sizes 3 to 6. The selected card rises and sits on top. Drag-up-to-play, the 220 px play lift and tap-to-zoom are unchanged. This replaces the sideways scroll. If P1's `battle_hand_fit` work has landed, reuse it.

### 5.4 Card zoom (mockups `C_Zoom`, `A_Zoom`, `B_Zoom`)

The card itself is today's zoom face (`CardBackView`), unchanged (D3). Each style changes the backdrop (a blurred room), a title strip and the two buttons.
- **Broadcast:** the title strip has gold lines.
- **Woodblock:** the card sits in an indigo frame.
- **Living Office:** a paper note under the card restates the "In this room" result and the energy cost.

The cue banner stays hidden while the zoom is open, paused rather than lost (P2 follow-up item 1).

### 5.5 Office styles shop (mockup `C_Styles`)

- **Tabs:** a new Marketplace tab, "Office styles", next to the existing items.
- **Contents:** one card per style, with a preview, a one-line description and a state: "In use", "Owned · Use this style", or "Unlock · price". A refused purchase shows its reason (Standard 2).
- **Footer:** "Once unlocked, change styles here or in Administration › Appearance and Music. Card fronts and backs look the same in every style."

## 7. Sizes and access (every style)

- **Touch targets:** at least 44 pt, which is 122 base px.
- **Text sizes:** follow T1's decision. Until then use today's theme sizes. The mockups were drawn at about 16 / 13 / 11 pt.
- **Contrast:** text at least 4.5:1 and icons at least 3:1, measured from pixels as `card_faces_test` does.
- **Large text:** every restyled screen must pass the P3 sweep at the Large text size.
- **Colour:** never the only signal. The seats carry numbers, and party colours sit beside party names.
