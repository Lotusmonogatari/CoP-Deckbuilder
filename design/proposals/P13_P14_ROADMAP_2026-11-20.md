# P13 and P14 roadmap: Office screens, then all the remaining screens (2026-11-20)

A draft for Cameron. Nothing here is built. It plans the execution of the two design packages `p13_office_screens_package.zip` and `p14_remaining_screens_package.zip` (both dated 2026-11-19).

## 1. Executive summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What are they? | A redesign of **38 screens** (6 in P13, 32 in P14) onto a few shared layouts, each drawn in all three styles (Living Office, Broadcast, Woodblock). Presentation only: no rules, numbers, IDs or schema change. |
| Do the specs match the game? | Yes. Every function, class and Text key the specs cite as existing was found in the code and in `strings.json` (21 in P13, 22 in P14). The base commit `215f3d28` is already in our history. Fonts and style tokens the mockups use are already in the project. |
| How big? | Far beyond 150 lines each. P13 is six commits, P14 seven. About 16 full `verify.sh` runs at about 9 minutes each, roughly 2.5 hours of gate time, before any fixes. |
| Biggest risk | `OfficeScreen.gd` is 4,383 lines, 40 test files mention it, and the 52 click tests find controls by node name and button text. Rebuilding screens can break tests that have nothing wrong with the game. |
| Order | P13 first (it builds the Office frame that P14 reuses), then P14. Each stops at a pushed branch; you review screenshots and say "merge". |
| What I need from you | Six decisions (section 4). Two are needed before P13 can start. |

| Risk | Handling |
|---|---|

| Style regressions (three styles, three text sizes) | Text sweep, `ui_target_audit.py` and contrast checks are part of each gate. Screenshots at 1080 x 2340, 1080 x 1920 and Large text. |
| Gate time | `verify.sh` is about 9 minutes and nothing may be edited while it runs. I will batch work between gates. |
| P14 is more than twice P13's size | Split into P14 (groups 1 to 3) and P14b (groups 4 to 7), as the prompt recommends. |
