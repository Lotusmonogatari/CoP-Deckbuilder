# District activities: a design for approval (2026-10-23)

Nothing here is built. Each activity is a single-stage level, a clone of the Office Hours room with its own pool of visitors, in the same shape as "Attend a Local Festival" (ST264). Content is draft for you to rewrite.

## Executive summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What are they | Four short, single-stage district outings. The player meets 6 to 10 constituents, answers dilemma questions, and earns small Constituency support, District Awareness and organization standing |
| Why | The weekend has only five levels. These give it about nine, and they give District Awareness a way to grow that is not a bill |
| Cost to build | No new code. Each is one Stages row, one Levels row and a visitor pool, exactly as the festival was |
| Needs from you | Pick which of the four to build, and the hours (default 2 to 4) |

## The four activities

| Activity | Hours | Visitors per visit | Pool | Best reward | Typical organizations pleased |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Constituency Walkabout | 2 | 6 | 14 street and shop-front locals: shopkeepers, commuters, a retired teacher, a delivery driver | District Awareness +2 | Neighborhood Associations, Taxpayers' Alliance, Commuters |
| Community Clinic Day | 3 | 6 | 14 patients, nurses, caregivers and a clinic director | Constituency support +1 to +2 | Medical Association, Social Care, Families |
| School Visit | 2 | 8 | 14 teachers, parents, a principal, a school-board member | Reputation +1 | Education organizations, Families, Students United |
| Farmers' Market Stall | 4 | 10 | 14 growers, a fisher, a co-op clerk, buyers | Funds (small) and Agricultural standing | Agricultural Cooperatives, Fisheries |

## How they work

| Rule | Draft |
|---|---|
| Room type | Office Hours clone (visitors with two dilemma questions each), same screen and engine |
| Calendar | Each costs its hours. On a weekday they fit in the 8-hour day, so they compete with bill levels. On a weekend they fit if the hours are left |
| Cooldown | 8 to 24 hours, like the festival, so the same one cannot be farmed |
| Weekend OK | Yes for all four. Together with LV83 to LV87 that makes nine weekend-capable levels |
| Content budget | The existing test: each choice 2 to 7 in total change, any gain costs something, at least one choice at +3 |
| Gating | Not gated. All four are open from the start, unlike half of the bill levels |
| Seasonal variants | Optional later: walkabout in snow, market in autumn, as with the festival |
