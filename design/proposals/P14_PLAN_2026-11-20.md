## 2. The four layouts

| Layout | New or reused | How it is built |
|---|---|---|
| Office frame | Reused from P13 | Staff Assignments, staff profile, Rhetoric Training, Your Deck, Inventory, Travel, Draft a Bill, Parties, Appearance and Music, How to Play, Characters. |
| **Pop-up card** | New | `PopupCard`: a header with a kind tag and a meta line (day, office, hours), a title, a scrolling body and a fixed button row, drawn by `PopupCardStyles` from the style tokens. It sits in an `Overlay` through a new `Overlay.open_card`, which draws the scrim (the room stays visible behind it) instead of P13's opaque ground. The Overlay keeps the way out: a pop-up that must be answered (an event, a Decision!, the stage outcome) stays non-dismissable exactly as it is today; one that may be closed (a promise list, a law) keeps its Back. |
| **Conversation** | Extends `VisitLayout` | Office Hours, member visit and lobbyist trade already share `VisitLayout`; the level intro joins them. The name plate, the question card under the picture and the full-width choices are added to it, not forked. |
| Scene | New, small | Stage transition, the Floor Vote header and New Game: a full-bleed picture with a card over the lower part. |

New style tokens (UI Styles tab rows, as P12 and P13 did): the pop-up scrim and the alarm tag colour in each style (six rows). The plate colour already exists as the P12 card colour.
