# Loops in the economy links

Generated by `tools/economy_map.py`; do not edit. A cycle with an even number of `-` links is **R** (reinforcing); an odd number is **B** (balancing). A gated link counts as the sign of its dependence on its active side (`g+` or `g-`). Mixed (`~`) links are left out.

## Flags

- **Unbraked R** (an R loop sharing no stock with any B loop): 0
- **Spiral** (an R loop through a gate's low side): 129
- **Dead ends** (no link out, or status unused): cosmetics, election_strength, segment_favorability
