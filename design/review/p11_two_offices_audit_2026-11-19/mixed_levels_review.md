## A rule that would settle them

If you want a rule rather than a call per level: **a Mixed level is District when it has no floor vote or floor debate and its constituent-facing rooms (Town Hall, Office Hours) outnumber its Capitol rooms.** Checked against these twelve, it moves only **LV62** (two constituent-facing rooms against one Capitol room). **LV12 and LV61 tie** (two against two), so a strict "outnumber" leaves them Capitol; "at least as many" would move them too. Every level with a floor vote or a floor debate stays Capitol. Say which you prefer and I will re-run the audit with it.
