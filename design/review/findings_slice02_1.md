Summary of patterns:
1. The topic-slot template breaks grammar in about 85 rows ("Our the vulnerable", "The regions is", "Our defenses deserves", one lowercase "our"); fixing it once in the template clears most of them.
2. The same lines are shared word for word across speakers in a committee (Earnest legacy rows, 9 Divisive and 8 Duplicitous lines), so consecutive opponents repeat each other; style labels in the oldest rows (OC109-117) are not audible.
3. Gain lines often respond to the player instead of boasting for the speaker, and party-topic slots ("the vulnerable", "order and custom") clash with the room or the speaker's office (War, Environment, Ministers).
