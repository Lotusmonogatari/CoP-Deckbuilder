Biggest patterns:
- The 440 topic rows are one template. The biggest risks are the missing bill scope (bills=None) and a real-world place name, "Hokkaido", in OC2557-OC2562.
- Grammar: singular org names get plural verbs ("are worried", "fear") while plural names get singular ones ("wants", "Does ... speak"). A number flag in the data would fix it.
- Stock Emotional and Appeal lines ("do not ask families to carry the cost", "are our neighbors") do not fit transparency bills, national orgs or Members of Parliament, and "This is not paperwork" collides with paperwork bills.
