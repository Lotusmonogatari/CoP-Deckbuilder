## The rule used

- A pledge ("I'll push for / fight for / back X", or "Yes" to "Will you push for X?") is a small promise: 1 hour within 48 game hours (one week), as Cameron set on 2026-10-31.
- A concrete follow-up ("I will ask the culture office", "I'll report it", "I'll speak at both") is the same promise; an evening event (a speech, a night ferry, a TV show, a workshop visit, a campus debate) is 2 hours.
- Something done on the spot costs now: VQ220 A pays a 20,000円 share, VQ84 D takes 1 hour.
- A disposition with nothing to deliver ("I'll vote on each bill on its merits", "I'll owe the voters") is reworded into the present tense and stays free.
- Every question keeps at least one free choice. Every new promise has its own Promise Kept Text.
