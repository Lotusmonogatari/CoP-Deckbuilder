ROWS READ: 169 of 169

Summary of the biggest patterns:
1. The five all-Japanese headlines break the English-first rule and some are not natural Japanese. Curly versus straight quotes is inconsistent across the group, and several lines run too long.
2. Conditions are wrong or misleading in places: Reputation = 50 exact (TK09-TK11), overlapping Funds bands that send opposite messages, and the Media Spokesperson "refuses to hire staff" line shown when the player cannot hire.
3. The 100 law headlines are five templates stamped on 50 bills, with no sources and some unsupported claims ("on the record", Agreements "enacted"). Two named characters (Yumi Satou, Rika Ashikaga) and a real institution (Cabinet Legislation Bureau) are not in the data.
