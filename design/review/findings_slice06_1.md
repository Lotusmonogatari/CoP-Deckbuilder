Summary of biggest patterns:
1. In the ST267 district set (VQ283-VQ306) and several festival rows, B/C responses answer a different choice (usually D), so the response does not match the pick. The count is about 13 rows (VQ283, 284, 286, 287, 288, 289, 292, 293, 294, 301, 304, 305, 306).
2. The organization-effect logic is mechanical. The best choice always carries an unexplained -1 cost, and the bad B choice carries an unexplained +1. BO23, BO24 and BO26 are used as generic tags, and a few go the wrong way (tax cut or relief angers the Taxpayers' Alliance).
3. Choices and reactions are formulaic and repetitive: the best option is always the longest and starts with "Support/Back/Fund", stock lines ("Do nothing", "It is the market") repeat, near-duplicate choices sit in the same question, and British vocabulary plus real-world places (Sapporo, Tokyo, prefecture) conflict with house style and canon.
