Three biggest patterns:
1. Four mass templates make up about 85% of the slice: 93 player thoughts, 119 SF01 "research is thin" lines, 100 SF01 Support/Oppose lines and 121 SF08 "Someone will ask why we should" lines. Each repeats its reply verbatim and never reacts to the specific topic.

3. Minor awkward phrasings and role overlaps in the hand-written SF01/SF02/SF08 rows (TD04, TD255, TD257, TD263, TD06, TD1608). Four bills (BI07, BI21, BI41, BI45) fall back to "this bill".
