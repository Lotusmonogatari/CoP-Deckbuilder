ROWS READ: 444 of 444

Biggest patterns:
1. Template grammar leaks: "Ordinary people is...", "Our towns requires...", "What does [plural problem] cost?", "who owns land near your harbor", "Let us keep [problem] between us" (rows listed above). These are A/B and fixable at the generator.
2. Heavy cross-speaker duplication: the ~15-sentence faction template and the six-reporter stock lines repeat verbatim, so named opponents do not sound distinct; journalists especially speak as a partisan faction ("our side", "public record" as a cause, weeping on air).
3. Bill cues have no consistent stance (critic and defender voices in one pool) and several real-world / sensitive references (Tomari, Chishima, US, Ainu grouped with wildlife) for Cameron to rule on.
