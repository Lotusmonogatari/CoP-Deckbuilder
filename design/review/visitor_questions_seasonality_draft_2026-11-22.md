# Visitor questions that may need a Seasons cell: a draft list (2026-11-22)

**Applied 2026-11-22 (Cameron approved Clear, Likely and Check as proposed, with one rule).** The rule: a question about a *general* matter also takes the season before (a Winter matter is asked in Autumn and Winter); a *temporal* matter about what is happening "now" (VQ230, VQ260, VQ270, VQ374 and similar) takes only its own season. The Proposed Seasons column below is the first draft; the workbook's Seasons cells hold what was applied. Originally: nothing in the workbook had changed. I scanned all 429 older visitor questions for season words and read the matches. Most hits were false (the verb "fall", "heat" in a political sense), so this is the list that survived. A season here means the question is only asked in that season's months.

- **Likely:** the subject is seasonal but the text does not say so.
- **Check:** it could be asked all year; your call.
