# Proposed Question Themes rebalance (draft, 2026-10-10)

**Status: APPLIED to the workbook on 2026-10-11 (105 of the 146 older themes changed; still a draft for Cameron to correct).** The tables below show the before/after at the time of the proposal; the 15 themes added later (Ainu, fisheries, commuter) are not in them. It replaced the Organisation column of the Question Themes tab. A strong answer on a theme pleases every organisation listed (the first is the primary); the per-stage swing cap per organisation is unchanged.

## Why

Today every one of the 146 themes pleases exactly one organisation, and 14 of the 30 organisations are never pleased by anything (BO11 and BO13 from before, plus the 12 new ones BO19-BO30), with Commuter Committee pleased once. Meanwhile National Media is pleased by 31 questions and the Chamber of Commerce by 23. That makes some parties (Keizaijiyuutou, Five Point Independents) easy to please and others (Yezo Heritage Party, Butsutou, Country Initiative) nearly impossible, whatever the player says.

## Notes and judgment calls

- Themes that are about the *process* of lawmaking (Definitions, Drafting, Scope, Metrics, Legality, Purpose) stay with the Labor Federation as before; they were probably a placeholder choice and are easy to move.
- Ainu-related themes (Identity, Consent, Local Consent, Representation, Equal rights, Integration, Access) are tied to the two Ainu organisations as a first draft; these touch the Yezo Heritage Party's core and are especially yours to correct.
- Party-tier organisations (Party Headquarters, Party Faction) keep their party-politics themes.
- Nothing is removed outright except moving the *primary* on the rows above; the old primary stays as a secondary wherever it still fits.
