Finding format:

## Store requirements I looked up (2026-11-10)

I searched for the current rules instead of relying on memory. Where I could only find secondary reporting, I say so; check the official page before acting.

| Store | Requirement found | Source | Status of source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Play | New apps and updates must target **Android 16 (API level 36)** from 2026-08-31. | [developer.android.com target SDK page](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk); [Play Console help](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/11926878) | Official pages returned by search; date confirmed by several secondary reports |
| Google Play | Apps targeting Android 15+ must support **16 KB memory page sizes** (from 2025-11-01). | [developer.android.com 16 KB guide](https://developer.android.com/guide/practices/page-sizes); [Godot forum thread](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/godot-and-google-play-policy-warning-about-16-kb-memory-page-size/120934) | Official guide exists; the date comes from secondary reports. Godot 4.5's upgrade notes mention 16 KB alignment work. |
| Google Play | **Personal developer accounts** created after 2023-11-13 must run a closed test with 12 testers for 14 continuous days before production access. Organization accounts are exempt. | [Play Console help 14151465](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465) | Official page returned; figures read from secondary summaries |
| Google Play | Size limits use compressed download size: feature modules 200 MB each, asset packs 1.5 GB each, 4 GB total. The **base module 200 MB** figure is confirmed only by Unity's documentation. | [Play Console help 9859372](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859372); [Unity manual](https://docs.unity3d.com/Manual/android-asset-packs-in-unity.html) | Partly official |
| Apple App Store | From 2026-04-28, uploads must be built with **Xcode 26 and the iOS 26 SDK**. | [Apple upcoming requirements](https://www.developer.apple.com/news/upcoming-requirements/) | Official |
| Steam Deck Verified | Default controller configuration must reach all content with no setting changes; glyphs must match the controller; on-screen keyboard appears for text entry; text readable at 30 cm, **smallest character at least 9 px high at 1280x800** (12 px recommended); supports 1280x800 or 1280x720; playable framerate by default. | [Steamworks Deck compatibility](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamdeck/compat); [Steamworks hardware compat](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamhardware/compat); [recommendations](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/steamdeck/recommendations) | Official |
| Steam | The content survey requires disclosing **generative AI content the player consumes** (pre-generated art, audio, text; and live-generated). Rewritten January 2026 to exempt internal tools. | [Notebookcheck report](https://www.notebookcheck.net/Steam-updates-AI-disclosure-form-requiring-developers-to-report-visible-and-in-game-AI-but-not-background-tools.1206103.0.html); [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/01/valve-tweak-steam-ai-disclosure-form-for-developers-to-clarify-its-for-content-consumed-by-players/) | Secondary only; I did not reach the Steamworks page |

**Not confirmed:** which Godot 4.x release's Android export template targets API 36 by default. Forum posts suggest Godot 4.5 targets API 35 and that 4.7 defaults to 36 ([Godot forum](https://forum.godotengine.org/t/how-to-target-latest-android-sdk/142631)). The target SDK can be raised in the export preset with a Gradle build, but I could not verify that 4.5.1 passes Play's check at 36.

## What I could not check in this phase

- Anything requiring a built app: actual download size, memory use, frame rate, startup time, back-button behavior, safe-area insets on a real notch, Xcode 26 compatibility, Play Console's 16 KB and target-API checks.
- The Play developer account type and the Apple and Steam account status.
- Rendering at 1280x800 (I computed the scale; I did not take a screenshot at that size, to keep the audit read-only and short).
